## Acknowledgements

The quasi-steady tether model follows Paul Williams, *Cable Modeling Approximations for Rapid Simulation*, Journal of Guidance, Control, and Dynamics, 40(7), 2017.

The tether model implemented here originates in earlier work on kite power systems at Delft University of Technology. This package depends on ModelingToolkit.jl and the wider SciML ecosystem.

The complete list of contributors is available in the [git history](https://github.com/ufechner7/Tethers.jl/graphs/contributors).
